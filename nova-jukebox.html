Why This Page Exists

A music upload page inside AP Advantage Player, not just another playlist

AP Jukebox gives the site a real music-upload landing page instead of burying songs as an afterthought. People can drag in a track, see it land on the shared shelf, stream what other visitors uploaded, and remove their own uploads later if they want.

That makes this page useful for searches around uploading songs, sharing tracks on a website, or exploring a community music shelf. It also broadens the site beyond pure games, which helps AP Advantage Player feel like a real creative destination rather than a one-format project list.

If someone lands here first and wants the bigger picture, the best follow-up link is the AP Advantage Player games guide, followed by the video side of the site in AP Clips.
