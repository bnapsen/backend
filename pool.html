Loaded cue control

Analog

Click or touch the table to aim, or grab the cue itself, then pull straight backward to load the shot and release to fire. Small movements near the cue ball are smoothed out.

Quick cue tip

Sight the line first, then either aim from in front of the cue ball or grab the cue stick itself and pull it straight back. Break shots still have the extra headroom to really open the rack.

Turn note

Pocket a scoring ball to stay at the table. Scratches and jammers pass control.
