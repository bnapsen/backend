Turbo Trail Rush

Hammer the throttle, manage turbo heat, cut across the lanes, and land clean off the biggest dirt ramps before the rival pack turns the whole track into a crash reel.

How it rides

Four lanes. Hot boost. No free landings.

  • W / Up accelerates and keeps the bike biting into the dirt.
  • S / Down settles the bike and scrubs speed before bad jumps.
  • A / D or Left / Right cut between lanes and nudge your angle in the air.
  • Shift / X hits turbo until the engine cooks.
